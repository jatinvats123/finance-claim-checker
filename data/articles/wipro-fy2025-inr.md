# Wipro's FY25: revenue flat, profit up sharply

Wipro reported revenue of ₹89,088 crore for the financial year ended March 31, 2025, which slipped 0.75% from ₹89,760 crore in the previous year.

Net profit attributable to shareholders rose 18.9% to ₹13,135 crore. Diluted earnings per share were ₹12.52.

Operating profit was ₹1,51,271 crore for the year. In dollar terms, revenue was $10.4 billion.

The IT services company said deal bookings remained steady.
