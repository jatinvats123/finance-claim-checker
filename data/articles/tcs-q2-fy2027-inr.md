# TCS Q2 FY27: revenue up 11%, profit up 15%

Tata Consultancy Services reported consolidated revenue from operations of ₹73,188 crore for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, up 11.2% from a year earlier.

Net profit attributable to shareholders rose 15% from a year earlier to ₹13,884 crore, and diluted earnings per share were ₹38.37. One brokerage note put net profit attributable to shareholders at ₹13,934 crore.

For the first half of the fiscal year, revenue was ₹1.45 lakh crore and diluted earnings per share were ₹72.57. On a standalone basis, net profit for the quarter was ₹12,400 crore.

Operating margin was 24.0%.
